First time, about 10 minutes

Install Joblin

You'll paste one line into Terminal. The installer downloads everything else by itself, including Ollama and the AI model. It never asks for your password.

A Mac (Apple chip or Intel) or Linux 8 GB memory or more 5 GB free disk space Internet for the downloads Google Chrome (recommended)
Not sure about your Mac? Click the Apple menu → About This Mac. It shows your chip and memory. For disk space, open System Settings → General → Storage.
1

Open Terminal

Terminal is an app that's already on every Mac. It lets you paste in commands.

  1. Press ⌘ Command + Space to open Spotlight search.
  2. Type Terminal.
  3. Press Return.
🔍TerminalApplications ›

A window opens with some text and a blinking cursor. That's it. Leave it open.

2

Copy and run the installer

  1. Click Copy on the box below.
  2. Click inside the Terminal window.
  3. Press ⌘ Command + V to paste.
  4. Press Return.
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vishinoo/joblin/main/install.sh | bash
What this does: downloads Joblin's installer from this GitHub page and runs it. It puts Joblin in a joblin folder in your home folder and Ollama in Applications. Nothing else on your Mac is changed.
3

Wait while it sets up (don't close the window)

You'll see these four steps go by. You don't have to type anything.

Terminal

👺 Step 1 of 4: Downloading Node.js (Joblin's engine)…
👺 Step 2 of 4: Downloading Joblin…
   Installing Joblin's parts (a minute or two)…
👺 Step 3 of 4: Downloading Ollama (the free AI brain)…

👺 All set! Joblin is opening now.
  • During step 3, the Ollama app opens. That's expected. If it asks to install its "command line tool", you can click Skip or Not now. You'll see a small llama icon in your menu bar at the top of the screen. That means Ollama is running.
  • Step 4 is the longest (a 2 GB download). It only happens once.
  • If something goes wrong, just paste the same line again and press Return. It skips what's already done.
4

Joblin opens. Follow his 5 setup screens.

Joblin opens in his own window. Click Continue after each screen:

  1. Your details: name, email, phone, school and country. Your country becomes the default place to search.
  2. Your resumes: upload your resume (PDF or Word). Joblin reads it and shows you what he found. Fix anything that looks wrong.
  3. Which positions: pick the kinds of roles you want, then tick the box confirming they're right.
  4. Connect your accounts: LinkedIn (to find more jobs) and Gmail (to send emails). Both are optional and can be done later. See step 5.
  5. How hands-on: pick Review each batch (recommended to start) or Autopilot.

Click Finish setup. Joblin asks if you want to find your first batch now. Click Find jobs now.

Next time, just double-click Joblin on your Desktop. More on that in Every day.
5

Connect Gmail (for coffee-chat emails)

You need this for Joblin to email people. Your Google account needs 2-Step Verification turned on.

  1. In Joblin, open Settings → Integrations.
  2. Click Connect Gmail.
  3. Sign in to Google in the window that opens.
  4. That's it. Joblin sets up sending by himself, and types in the security codes job sites email you.
Prefer to set it up yourself with an app password?
  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  2. Type Joblin as the name and click Create.
  3. Copy the 16-letter code.
  4. In Joblin's Settings → Integrations, paste your Gmail address and the code, then click Test connection.

LinkedIn (optional): in Settings → Integrations, click Connect next to LinkedIn and sign in. Joblin remembers the login like a normal browser.

Every day

Using Joblin

1

Make sure Ollama is running

Look for the llama icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen. If it's there, you're good. If not, open Ollama in one of two ways:

  • Open Ollama from your Applications folder, or
  • paste this in Terminal:
open -a Ollama

To check it's running, paste this. It should print the line below.

curl http://127.0.0.1:11434
Ollama is running
2

Start Joblin

Easiest: double-click Joblin on your Desktop. A Terminal window opens, then Joblin's window.

Or start him from Terminal:

cd ~/joblin && PATH="$HOME/joblin/.runtime/node/bin:$PATH" npm start

Rather use a normal browser tab? Start him without his own window:

cd ~/joblin && PATH="$HOME/joblin/.runtime/node/bin:$PATH" npm run background

Then open http://localhost:4321 in Chrome.

Keep the Terminal window open while Joblin works. It's his engine. Closing it stops him.
3

Tell Joblin what to find

Click Joblin's face in the bottom-right corner to open the chat, and type what you want, for example:

Finance internships in Edmonton

He saves that as your search and starts looking. Or just click Find jobs now at the top to run your saved search. You'll see his progress at the top: checking job boards, searching LinkedIn, writing resumes and cover letters.

4

Review the batch and apply

  1. Open Review batch in the sidebar.
  2. Click any job to see why it fits ("What they want, and your proof") and its cover letter. Use Edit text to change anything.
  3. Tick the jobs you want, or click Select all.
  4. Click Apply to N, then confirm.

Joblin applies one by one in a hidden browser, so you can keep using your computer. Watch it on the Applications page: each job goes from In queue to Applied.

"Needs you" or "Questions": if an application asks something new, it shows up under Questions. Answer it once and Joblin reuses that answer for every similar question.
5

Coffee-chat emails go out by themselves

After each application, Joblin finds people at that company and writes them a short note asking for a quick chat. Open Outreach to see them:

  • Scheduled emails go out slowly during business hours (40 a day at most, a few minutes apart).
  • Click an email to read it. Click Cancel to stop one, or Pause sending to stop them all.
  • When someone replies, the email shows Replied with a preview of what they said. If nobody replies, Joblin follows up once after 5 days.
6

Switch on Autopilot (optional)

Once you trust his letters, go to Settings → Automation and choose Autopilot. Joblin then finds jobs, applies and emails on a schedule (every 6 hours by default) without asking. Keep Joblin and Ollama running for this to work.

7

Stop Joblin

Click the Terminal window that's running Joblin and press Control + C, or just close that window. Joblin closes his browser and stops. Start him again any time with step 2.

Ollama commands

Ollama is the free AI that writes your letters. The installer sets it up, so you only need these if something's off.

Start Ollama (Mac)

open -a Ollama

Start Ollama (Linux)

ollama serve

Check it's running (should print "Ollama is running")

curl http://127.0.0.1:11434

Download Joblin's model (about 2 GB). This works even if you skipped Ollama's command line tool.

curl http://127.0.0.1:11434/api/pull -d '{"model":"qwen2.5:3b"}'

See which models you have

curl http://127.0.0.1:11434/api/tags

Smarter letters on a Mac with 16 GB+ memory: download the bigger model, then pick it in Settings → AI model

curl http://127.0.0.1:11434/api/pull -d '{"model":"qwen2.5:7b"}'
Have Ollama's command line tool? Then the shorter versions work too: ollama pull qwen2.5:3b, ollama list, and ollama rm qwen2.5:7b to delete a model.

Manual install, every command

Same result as the one-line installer, done by hand. Run these one at a time, in order.

1

Install Node.js 20 or newer

Download the LTS installer from nodejs.org and open it. Then check it worked (should print v20 or higher):

node -v
2

Install Ollama and the model

Download Ollama from ollama.com/download, drag it to Applications, and open it once. Then:

curl http://127.0.0.1:11434/api/pull -d '{"model":"qwen2.5:3b"}'
3

Install git (Mac)

If you've never used git, this pops up Apple's installer. Click Install and wait.

xcode-select --install
4

Download Joblin

git clone https://github.com/vishinoo/joblin.git ~/joblin
5

Install Joblin's parts

cd ~/joblin && npm install && npx playwright install chromium
6

Start Joblin

cd ~/joblin && npm start

Joblin opens his window and walks you through the setup screens.

Reference

Updating

Joblin checks for new versions by himself. When there's one, an Update available card appears in the sidebar. Click Update now. He downloads it and restarts in a few seconds.

Or update from Terminal by running the installer again:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vishinoo/joblin/main/install.sh | bash
Updates never touch your stuff. Your settings, resumes, letters, applications and passwords are kept.
Privacy

Your data stays on your laptop.

There is no Joblin server and no Joblin account. Nobody, including the person who made Joblin, can see your resume, applications or emails.

Where everything is kept

All of it is in the joblin folder in your home folder. In Finder, press ⌘ + Shift + H to find it.

WhatWhere
Your details and settingsconfig.json
Your resumesresumes/
Cover letter templatestemplates/
Applications, emails, saved answersdata/jobert.db
Every resume and letter Joblin madedata/output/, one folder per job, as PDFs
Gmail and AI passwords/keys.env, never shown in the app
LinkedIn and Google sign-insdata/, the way a browser remembers a login

What leaves your laptop

Only what's needed for what you asked:

  • Applications go to the companies you approved, on their own job sites.
  • Emails go from your Gmail to the people on the Outreach page.
  • Searches for jobs and for who works at each company (company sites, LinkedIn, public directories).
  • An update check asks GitHub if there's a new version. Nothing about you is sent.

What never leaves

  • The AI's work. With Ollama, your resume, letters and emails are written on your laptop.
  • Your inbox. Joblin reads your last week of email to spot replies and interview invites, and sorts them on your laptop.
  • Your history. Applications, answers and notes stay in the folder above.
  • Tracking. There's none. No analytics, no telemetry.
If you switch to Claude, OpenAI or Gemini in Settings → AI model, the job posting, your relevant resume details and the emails Joblin sorts are sent to that company to do the writing. Stay on Ollama to keep everything on your laptop.

Uninstall

  1. Stop Joblin (close his Terminal window).
  2. Drag the joblin folder in your home folder, and Joblin on your Desktop, to the Trash. Or paste: 
    rm -rf ~/joblin ~/Desktop/Joblin.command
  3. To remove the AI too: quit Ollama from the llama icon in the menu bar, drag Ollama from Applications to the Trash, then: 
    rm -rf ~/.ollama
  4. Remove Joblin's Gmail access at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and myaccount.google.com/permissions.
Help

Troubleshooting

"command not found: npm" or "command not found: node"

The installer keeps its own copy of Node inside the Joblin folder. Start Joblin with this command, which points to it:

cd ~/joblin && PATH="$HOME/joblin/.runtime/node/bin:$PATH" npm start

Or just double-click Joblin on your Desktop.

Joblin says the writing model isn't ready

Ollama isn't running or the model isn't downloaded. Run these one at a time:

open -a Ollama
curl http://127.0.0.1:11434/api/pull -d '{"model":"qwen2.5:3b"}'

Then in Joblin, open Settings → AI model and click Test.

Joblin's window didn't open

He's probably running anyway. Open http://localhost:4321 in Chrome.

"Port 4321 is already in use" or "EADDRINUSE"

Joblin is already running in another Terminal window. Use that one, or stop the old one with:

lsof -ti tcp:4321 | xargs kill
The installer stopped halfway

Paste the install line again. It picks up where it left off and never deletes your data.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vishinoo/joblin/main/install.sh | bash
An application says "Needs a hand" or "Apply yourself"

Some sites need something Joblin can't do alone, like an unusual question or a captcha. Open the job from Applications, answer anything under Questions, then click Try again. For "Apply yourself" jobs, click the job title to open the posting and apply there.

Letters are slow

The AI runs on your laptop, so older machines take longer. Close other heavy apps. Joblin keeps working in the background, so you don't need to watch it.

Does it work on Windows?

Not directly yet. Install WSL (Windows' built-in Linux), open it, and run the install line there.

Can I get in trouble with LinkedIn or job sites?

Some sites don't allow automated applications, and LinkedIn can limit accounts that act like bots. Joblin goes slowly on purpose, but start in Review mode, keep batches small, and keep the email limit low.
