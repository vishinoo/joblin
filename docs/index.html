How to use Joblin

Joblin's day goes: find jobs, write applications, wait for your OK, apply, email the recruiters, watch for replies.

1. Find jobs now

Click Find jobs now at the top. Joblin searches, picks the best matches, and writes a resume and cover letter for each.

2. Review the batch

Open Review batch. Read or edit each letter, then approve the ones you like. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

3. He applies

Joblin fills in each application in a hidden browser, so you can keep using your Mac. Follow along on Applications.

4. Coffee-chat emails

On Outreach, see the emails Joblin has queued to recruiters. They go out slowly, during business hours, and you can cancel any of them.

5. Answer his questions

When an application asks something new, it lands in Questions. Answer it once and Joblin reuses that answer forever.

6. Try Autopilot

Once you trust his letters, switch to Autopilot in Settings and Joblin finds and applies on a schedule.

Connecting Gmail (2 minutes)

Joblin sends from your own Gmail using an app password: a special code that only lets Joblin send and read mail. It's not your normal password, and you can cancel it any time.

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account, if it isn't already on.
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, type Joblin as the name, and click Create.
  3. Copy the 16-letter code Google shows you.
  4. In Joblin, open Settings → Integrations → Gmail, paste your Gmail address and the code, and click Test connection.

Updates

When there's a new version, an Update available card shows up in the sidebar. Click Update now and Joblin downloads it and restarts. Your settings, resumes and applications are never touched by an update.

Your data stays on your laptop.

There is no Joblin server and no Joblin account. Nobody, including the person who made Joblin, can see your resume, your applications or your emails.

What leaves your laptop

Only what's needed for what you asked Joblin to do:

  • Your applications go to the companies you approved, through their own job sites.
  • Your emails go from your Gmail to the recruiters you see on the Outreach page.
  • Searches for job listings and for who's hiring (company sites, LinkedIn, public search engines and directories).
  • An update check asks GitHub if there's a newer Joblin. Nothing about you is sent.

What never leaves

  • The AI's work. With Ollama, your resume, letters and emails are written on your laptop.
  • Your inbox. Joblin reads your last week of email to spot replies, interview invites and rejections, and sorts them on your laptop.
  • Your history. Your applications, answers and notes are only stored in the folder above.
  • Usage tracking. There's none. No analytics, no telemetry.
If you switch to Claude, OpenAI or Gemini in Settings → AI model, the job posting, your relevant resume details and the emails Joblin sorts are sent to that company to do the writing. Stay on Ollama to keep everything local.

Deleting everything

  1. Close Joblin (close its Terminal window).
  2. Drag the joblin folder from your home folder, and the Joblin launcher on your Desktop, to the Trash.
  3. To remove the AI too, quit Ollama, then drag Ollama from Applications to the Trash.
  4. Cancel the Gmail app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Joblin is open source under the MIT license, and Ollama's AI runs on your laptop for free. You only pay if you choose to plug in a paid AI like Claude or OpenAI.

Will it apply to jobs I don't want?

Not in Review each batch mode, which is the default: Joblin prepares applications and waits for you to approve each one. Only Autopilot applies on its own, and you have to switch that on.

Does it make things up on my resume?

No. Joblin only uses bullet points from the resume you gave it, and it rejects sentences that invent numbers or skills. You can still read and edit every letter before it's sent.

Is my laptop fast enough?

Most Macs from the last few years with 8 GB of memory are fine. Letters take a little longer on older machines. If yours has 16 GB or more, you can pick a smarter model in Settings → AI model.

Can I get in trouble with LinkedIn or job sites?

Some sites don't allow automated applications, and LinkedIn can limit accounts that act like bots. Joblin goes slowly and acts like a person, but use it sensibly: start in Review mode, keep batches small, and keep the email limit low.

Something broke. What do I do?

Paste the install line again: it repairs and updates Joblin without touching your data. If it's still broken, open an issue on GitHub and include what the Terminal window says.

Does it work on Windows?

Not directly yet. Install WSL (Windows' built-in Linux) and run the install line inside it.
